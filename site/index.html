01 / THE ASSIGNMENT

Another failed attempt.
A new mission.

Originally released in Spanish, MrNightology's Pokémon Team Rocket: Jessie & James Edition puts you in James's shoes alongside Jessie and Meowth.

After yet another failed attempt to catch Pikachu, a Delibird turns up with a letter from Giovanni. The trio head back to Kanto and take on a new set of Team Rocket missions.

Steal Pokémon and items, investigate suspicious places and interfere with other people's plans. I won't spoil the missions themselves. That's half the fun.
